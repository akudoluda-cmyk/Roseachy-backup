Recycle Bin & Backup

Recycle bin — deleted transactions
Data safety / backup
Full Backup includes stock, saved days, Market Desk, notes (including deleted notes), current draft, recycle bins and settings. The Stock tab’s Working-State Backup is for current inventory + current-day work and is not a replacement for a Full Backup.
⚠ Roseachy keeps your records in this phone’s private app storage (IndexedDB). Closing, restarting or updating the app never erases them, but clearing Chrome/site/app data, or uninstalling/removing the app’s data, can. Export a Full Backup regularly and copy it off the phone (Drive, email or a computer).
